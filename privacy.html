Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data IDFINITY collects, why, and the choices you have. We follow the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (Malaysia).

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, and if you sign in with Google, the name and profile picture Google shares with us. Passwords are stored encrypted by our sign-in provider; we never see them.
  • Card details you choose to add: name, photo, job title, company, location, short bio, tags, phone, WhatsApp, email and links.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and time of request) for security and to keep the Service running.

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. We only store what is needed to keep you signed in.

2. How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To show your card to people who open your link or scan your QR code.
  • To keep the Service secure and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We don't sell or rent your personal data, and we don't use it for advertising.

3. What is public

Everything you put on your card is public to anyone with your link or QR code, and they can save it to their phone. Your account email stays private unless you also add it to your card.

4. Who processes your data

We use trusted providers to run the Service:

  • Supabase for accounts, the card database and photo storage (servers in Singapore).
  • Netlify to host the website.
  • Google if you choose to sign in with Google, and Google Fonts to display the site's font.

Because these providers operate outside Malaysia, your data may be transferred and stored abroad. They protect it under their own security and privacy commitments.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and card until you delete them. When you delete your card, it is removed from public view straight away and from our database. To delete your whole account, email us and we will remove it within 30 days.

6. Your rights

You can see and edit your card details at any time from your editor, and delete your card yourself. You can also ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete your account by emailing muhdyusry.abdullah@gmail.com.

7. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, and database rules make sure only you can change your own card. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password.

8. Children

IDFINITY is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. Important changes will be shown on the site.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: muhdyusry.abdullah@gmail.com.
