About IDFINITY

IDFINITY is a free digital business card. You make your card once, share one link or QR code, and people save your details to their phone with a single tap.

Why we built it

Paper business cards run out, get lost, and go out of date the day your job title changes. A digital card is always with you, always current, and costs nothing to print.

What you get

  • Your own link, like idfinity.netlify.app/card/your-name
  • A QR code you can print, or show on your phone
  • A "Save to contacts" button that works on iPhone and Android
  • A phone wallpaper image with your QR code
  • Your photo, role, company, phone, email and links in one place

Free, really

IDFINITY is free to use. There is no app to install, no ads on your card, and we don't sell your data.
